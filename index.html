OCTOPUS TARIFFS EXPLAINED

Which Octopus tariff is right for you?

Octopus has more tariffs than most suppliers. Here's the plain-English version, so you know what to pick when you sign up. The £50 referral credit applies whichever one you choose.

Simple tariffs (no smart meter needed)

BEST FOR: KEEPING IT SIMPLE

Flexible Octopus

Variable electricity and gas. Prices move every three months with the wholesale market, and there are no exit fees, so you can leave or change tariff any time.

BEST FOR: BUDGET CERTAINTY

Fixed Octopus

Locks your rates for 12 or 18 months, so bills don't jump if prices rise. Exit fees may apply if you leave early.

Smart tariffs (smart meter required)

EV OWNERS

Intelligent Octopus Go

Octopus smart-charges your car at a very low rate, and your whole home gets six cheap hours every night. Needs a compatible EV or charger.

EV OWNERS

Octopus Go

Five hours of very cheap electricity every night for your home and car. The pick if your EV or charger doesn't work with Intelligent Go.

SOLAR + BATTERY

Octopus Flux

Cheap overnight rates to fill your home battery, plus good rates for selling surplus energy back to the grid.

FLEXIBLE USERS & BATTERIES

Agile Octopus

Prices change through the day, following the wholesale market. Great if you can shift usage away from the evening peak, such as home workers or battery owners.

FLEXIBLE USERS

Octopus Tracker

One price per day that follows wholesale costs, for electricity and gas. New customers usually join on Flexible first, then switch to Tracker.

HEAT PUMPS

Cosy Octopus

Three cheap periods every day, timed around how heat pumps run. For heat pump homes without a compatible EV.

STORAGE HEATERS

Snug Octopus

Six cheap hours each night plus an afternoon boost to top up storage heaters. Needs a compatible second-generation smart meter.

SOLAR EXPORT

Outgoing Octopus

Export tariffs that pay you for the solar power you send back to the grid. Outgoing and Agile Outgoing can be paired with Go and Intelligent Go.

Tariff details summarised from Octopus Energy's own tariff pages, November 2026. Availability and rates vary by region, so check the details on octopus.energy/tariffs when you sign up.

Quick questions

Is this an official Octopus Energy site?

No. It's a personal referral page run by Dominik. The links take you to Octopus Energy's own website, where you sign up as normal.

Does using the code cost me anything?

No. Your tariff and prices are the same as signing up directly. The referral just adds the credit.

When do I get the £50?

Octopus adds it once your switch is complete and your first Direct Debit has gone through. Check their current referral terms on octopus.energy for the exact details.

Does the person who referred me get anything?

Yes — Octopus gives £50 credit to both sides, so Dominik gets £50 too. It costs you nothing extra. Thanks if you use it!

Do I need a smart meter?

Not to switch or to get the £50. You only need one for the smart tariffs like Agile, Go, Intelligent Go, Cosy and Flux.

Which tariff should I choose?

EV at home: Intelligent Octopus Go. Heat pump: Cosy. Solar and a battery: Flux. Can shift your usage around: Agile or Tracker. Just want it simple: Flexible or Fixed. See the tariff guide above.
